## What's new in 1.0

- **Several Claude accounts, side by side.** Each account has its own sign-in and its own window.
- **`claude://` links go to the right account.** Sign-in callbacks and "open in app" links are offered to you in the app window instead of landing in a random window.
- **Launchers** for Dock, Spotlight and Raycast, with a coloured badge per account.
- **Picks up earlier setups.** Existing `Claude-<name>` data folders are offered as accounts, no new sign-in needed.
- **Updates itself.** A daily check, signed and notarized releases, and a "Check for Updates…" menu item.
- A butter-yellow mascot who keeps you company.
